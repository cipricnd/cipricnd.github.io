Car Time Trial – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 (updated: Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics)

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the mobile game Car Time Trial (the “Game”). The Game is developed by CND Mobile Games, operated by Ciprian Daniel Corondan, Romania (“we”, “us”), who is responsible for the Game within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Summary

  • The Game has no user accounts and we do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • The Game uses Google Firebase to understand how the Game is played (Analytics) and to receive reports when it crashes (Crashlytics).
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown and before gameplay statistics are collected, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the use of your data for targeted advertising at any time with PRIVACY CHOICES in the Game’s Settings.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (completed levels, best times, medals, the recording of your best lap used for the “ghost” car, coins, cars and upgrades) and your settings (volume, graphics quality, vibration and display options) locally on your device. This information is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data in your device settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, to show ads: an optional rewarded ad (“Watch ad ×2 coins”) and occasional full-screen ads between races. To deliver ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • information about the ads shown and your interactions with them (for example, views and taps).

Google uses this information to show ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud and, where you have given consent, to show ads personalised to your interests. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, to understand how the Game is played so we can improve it, for example which levels are too hard. It records:

  • game events: levels started, finished or abandoned, finish times and medals, coins earned and spent (for example on cars and upgrades) and rewarded ads watched;
  • an app instance identifier, the advertising ID and session information (when and how long the Game is used);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, country or city derived from the IP address, and the source the Game was installed from.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, these statistics are collected only after you consent in the consent message; until then the Game starts with statistics turned off. Analytics data is linked to an app instance identifier, not to your name, and user-level data is kept for 2 months (the Google Analytics retention setting we use). See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the Game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report so we can fix the problem. The report contains technical information: what went wrong in the code, the screen of the Game where it happened, device model, operating system version, app version, free memory and storage, and a random installation identifier. It does not contain your name or contact details. Crash reports are sent based on our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working and are kept for 90 days.

Your consent and choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game shows a consent message when it starts, using Google’s User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent management platform). You can consent, refuse, or choose individual purposes and partners. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: open Settings in the Game’s main menu and tap PRIVACY CHOICES.

On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices).

The legal basis for personalised advertising, gameplay statistics and for storing or accessing information on your device is your consent. Basic ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and crash reports rely on legitimate interests, as listed in the consent message.

Your privacy rights in the United States

Residents of US states with consumer privacy laws (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Virginia and others) have the right to know what personal information is collected about them, to access, correct or delete it, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information and of targeted advertising.

We do not sell personal information for money. However, allowing Google AdMob and its partners to use device identifiers to show personalised ads may be considered “selling” or “sharing” personal information, or “targeted advertising”, under these laws. In the last 12 months the Game has collected, through Google: identifiers (advertising ID, app instance ID, IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (gameplay events, ad interactions), approximate location derived from the IP address, and device information, all as described above.

Your Privacy Choices: to opt out, open Settings in the Game and tap PRIVACY CHOICES (the button appears where these laws apply). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. To exercise your other rights, or to appeal a decision about your request, email us at the address below; we respond within 45 days. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

Game engine

The Game is built with the Unity engine. The Unity engine may send anonymous technical statistics about the device (such as hardware and operating system information) to Unity Technologies. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network access – to load ads and the consent message and to send statistics and crash reports.
  • Advertising ID (and Android’s privacy-preserving advertising APIs) – used by AdMob and Analytics as described above.
  • Install referrer – lets Analytics know which store or campaign the Game was installed from.
  • Keep awake / background work – lets Firebase finish sending statistics and reports.
  • Vibration – short vibrations during races; you can turn them off in the Game’s Settings.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 years of age (or under the minimum age for digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and international transfers

We do not keep personal data about you on our own servers. Statistics and crash reports are stored by Google (Firebase) on our behalf for the periods described above; other data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s own policies. Google may process data outside the European Economic Area; where it does, it relies on safeguards approved by the European Commission, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw your consent at any time. Statistics and crash reports are linked only to random identifiers on your device, so to help with a request we may ask you for details from the Game; for advertising data you can also contact Google directly. Contact us and we will help. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority – in Romania, the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP, www.dataprotection.ro).

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
